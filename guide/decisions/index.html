    Clones and K¶

    popgen pauses twice, where the right answer depends on your data: the clone threshold and the number of genetic clusters, K. In both cases comRAD lays out the evidence, and you answer in the config, so the decision is recorded with the run. If you already know the answer, put it in the config before running and the pause is skipped.

    Pause 1: clones¶

    Clonal organisms put several samples of one genotype in a collection. Keeping them all inflates relatedness and skews allele frequencies, so the clonegroups step finds clone groups and filterbams keeps the best-covered sample of each.

    Which samples go in. A sample with almost no data looks close to everything in an IBS tree and pulls unrelated samples together. Samples covering less than CLONE_MINQ (0.20) of sites at QDEPTHx (5x) are left out of the clone step. They would fall below MINQ (0.25) afterwards in any case. The diary names them, and angsd/clones/<SP>CloneLowCoverage.txt lists them. Check that none of your technical replicates are among them.

    The distance. Two ways to measure how different two samples are:

    • Genotype distance: the share of sites where the two samples' genotype calls differ, among sites both are called at with high confidence (probability above 0.99, or 0.95). Clones and replicates sit near 0, whatever the species' heterozygosity.
    • IBS (1 - IBS): one random read per sample and site. It works at very low coverage, but two libraries of one heterozygous genotype differ at about half its heterozygous sites, so clones of a highly heterozygous species sit far from 0 and close to their relatives.

    With CLONE_METRIC = auto (the default) comRAD uses genotype distance when the data support it: at least 90% of pairs share 1000 or more confident sites, and the replicates are tight (their largest distance at most 0.15 of the median distance). It tries 0.99, then 0.95, and otherwise uses IBS. The diary and <SP>CloneMetric.txt give the numbers behind the choice. geno or ibs forces one; both uses genotypes and also writes the IBS tree, with the pairs the two group differently.

    In simulations with clones, replicates, parents and offspring, full, half and selfed sibs at about 7x, genotype distance found every clone with no relative merged, including a large clone of a highly heterozygous genet; IBS merged parent-offspring and selfed-sib pairs and missed that clone. At about 2.5x there are too few confident calls, auto falls back to IBS, and IBS still finds the clones but can merge close relatives: check the borderline joins.

    The cut. Technical replicates are known clones, so they show how different two libraries of one genotype can look. CLONE_CUT = auto places the cut:

    • for genotype distance, at the bottom of the widest gap between joins that starts within three times the replicates' largest distance (clones sit just above the replicates; relatives are much further up);
    • for IBS, at the deepest join within any replicate set times TREE_BUFFER (1.10), the method of treeTrimmer.

    gap or replicates forces one rule; a number sets the cut yourself (in the units of the distance used, given in <SP>CloneMetric.txt).

    The run pauses after drawing angsd/<SP>CloneDend.pdf: the tree, technical replicates in bold, and the cut as a red line (with genotype distance, <SP>CloneDendIBS.pdf shows the IBS tree for comparison). Look for a gap between the clone-level pairs (replicates and true clones, near the bottom) and everything else. If the red line sits in that gap, accept it. If not, set your own.

    nano popgen.config      # accept:  CLONE_PAUSE = no       or your own:  CLONE_CUT = 0.08
comrad run popgen

    Checks that come with it:

    • Replicate check. <SP>CloneReplicates.txt gives each replicate's nearest sample and the nearest sample outside its set. A replicate whose nearest sample is outside its own clone group gives a WARNING: a mislabelled or failed library.
    • Borderline joins. <SP>CloneBorderline.txt lists joins grouped as clones but more than 1.5 times the replicates' distance, and the nearest join left ungrouped, with each side's coverage. A weak library on one side points to a clone; good coverage on both, to a close relative.
    • Too many clones. If the clone groups would drop more than CLONE_MAXDROP (half) of the samples, the run stops. That is a cut or data problem, not a result.
    • Clone-free check. After genotyping, the genotype step draws angsd/<SP>CloneFreeDend.pdf for the kept samples, with the same distance and cut, and warns about any pair still below it.

    Replicates with no reads, or left out for low coverage, are warned about and left out; a replicate set needs two samples to count. If no set has two, set CLONE_CUT to a number.

    Pause 2: K¶

    admix runs NGSadmix at every K from 1 to MAXK, ADMIX_REPS times each, and evalAdmix on the best run of each K. The K step then gives five reads on K, in the diary and in run/popgen/K.log:

    note  K: evalAdmix elbow K = 2 (residual RMSD against K)
note  K: PCAngsd K = 2 (eigenvectors kept by its MAP test + 1; ...)
note  K: residual-block K = 2 (smallest K whose worst cluster-block mean |residual| < 0.01; plots: ngsAdmix/sintResiduals.pdf)
note  K: k-means BIC K = 2 (lowest BIC of k-means on the PCA scores, as adegenet find.clusters; table in ngsAdmix/sint_bic.txt)
note  K: runs in the dominant mode per K: 1:20/20 2:20/20 3:18/20 4:9/20
    read what it is
    evalAdmix elbow where the residual curve flattens. A geometric rule: it always returns something, and never K = 1 or MAXK
    PCAngsd the number of eigenvectors PCAngsd's MAP test kept, plus one (Meisner & Albrechtsen 2018); its own admixture at that K is the bottom row of ngsAdmix/<SP>Admix.pdf
    k-means BIC k-means clustering of the PCA scores, K with the lowest BIC (the find.clusters step of DAPC; Jombart et al. 2010). Independent of NGSadmix. Like PCAngsd, it also answers to families and batches
    residual-block K from the evalAdmix residuals in ngsAdmix/<SP>Residuals.pdf. Each sample is placed in its main cluster at MAXK; a K that lumps two real lineages leaves positive residuals within them. This is the smallest K with no block mean above RESID_MAX. It cannot tell you K is too high, and the cut-off is a judgement call: look at the plots
    mode support how many of the NGSadmix runs agree at each K. Low support means that K is not a stable solution 
    nano popgen.config      # K = 2
comrad run popgen

    K = 1 (no structure) is allowed: every sample is one lineage, FST between lineages is skipped (FST among sampling groups still runs), and demography does not apply. At K ≥ 2, the run stops at assign if fewer than two lineages have pure samples, or any has fewer than MINPURE.

    After K¶

    • Lineages. Each sample with at least ASSIGN (0.90) ancestry from one cluster belongs to that lineage; the rest are Admixed and stay out of the per-lineage statistics.
    • Kin. NGSremix estimates relatedness; for each pair at or above KIN (0.125), one sample is dropped: the one in most remaining pairs, then the one with less coverage.
    • Outliers. A PCAngsd selection scan within each lineage; SNPs with FDR < 0.05 on any PC are outliers, and their tags are removed from the SFS.
    • Neutral check. Structure is rerun without outliers or kin: PCAngsd, and NGSadmix with evalAdmix at every K from 1 to MAXK, with the same reads on K as at the pause (ngsAdmix/<SP>NeutralAdmix.pdf, <SP>NeutralResiduals.pdf). Close kin can pull PCs and clusters of their own and leave residual blocks, so this is K without them. If none of the elbow, PCAngsd, residual-block and k-means BIC K gives the K you chose, that is a WARNING. The lineage calls at your K are then compared with the full run's; a mismatch is a WARNING too, with the table in ngsAdmix/<SP>NeutralCompare.txt. Either way, change K and comrad from popgen assign if the kin-free run says otherwise.
